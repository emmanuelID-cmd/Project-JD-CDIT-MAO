Leave forms are conditional. Speak with your employer or HR before downloading or completing FMLA/PFL forms. A blank form does not confirm eligibility or approval. Potential supporting items, only when requested, may include your employee identifier; an appropriate provider certification or other relevant documentation; relationship or qualifying-event records when applicable; insurance information relevant to covered visits; and other documents named by HR or the insurer. Requirements vary. Send only requested items through an approved department channel.

Personal documents: Personal documents are not retained in ActionRelay ticket or automated-processing records and are not used for automated review. In a connected workflow, documents go only to the department responsible for the request and are used only for that department’s review. This local synthetic build has no real department delivery and does not accept document uploads. Do not attach real IDs, medical records, tax forms, or work-authorization records to this prototype.
