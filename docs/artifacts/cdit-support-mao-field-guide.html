Project at a glance

Proposed assistant for Tier 1 staff at a clinical-trial-management SaaS provider. It organizes cases, finds approved guidance, and prepares drafts for human review.

PRD companion

Problem

Specialists classify requests, meet SLAs, troubleshoot within policy, document interactions, and escalate beyond Tier 1. Baseline workload and resolution data are unknown.

Approach

A coordinator routes bounded triage, retrieval, troubleshooting, escalation, and documentation roles. Results show evidence, uncertainty, and proposed next steps.

Prototype boundary

Synthetic tickets and knowledge only. No Salesforce connection, customer messaging, account changes, PHI, or production authentication.

User needs are the acceptance lensSuccess means staff can prepare accurate, timely work and verify it. A generated answer alone is not success.

Proposed stack

Python 3.13 · FastAPI · Pydantic · SQLite · local versioned retrieval · one model adapter.

Unresolved

CDIT product modules, SLA definitions, escalation matrix, approved support documents, production identity and data controls, and lane owner confirmation.

Proposed architecture

Coordinator-managed blackboard state joins bounded agents, policy checks, approved retrieval, and human decisions. Planning architecture only; production integrations remain out of scope.

Trust boundaryTickets, attachments, retrieved passages, model outputs, and tool results are untrusted data. They cannot change policy, grant permissions, or authorize actions.
A0–A1 · INTAKE

Authenticate & minimize

Validate actor, role, case scope, schema, and data class. Isolate untrusted instructions and minimize context.

BLACKBOARD

Versioned shared state

Coordinator-owned case/run record. Agent contributions retain provenance, confidence, uncertainty, validation, and timestamps.

A2–A6 · WORKERS

Bounded agents

Triage, knowledge, troubleshooting, escalation, and documentation use narrow inputs and allowlisted tools.

A7 · REVIEW

Human disposition

Authorized staff approve, edit, reject, escalate, and perform consequential actions.

Direct pipeline

Coordinator → Triage → Knowledge → Troubleshooting → Escalation → Documentation → Coordinator validation → Human. Each stage consumes acknowledged upstream versions; failed checks stop downstream work.

Parallel analysis

After validated intake and triage, Knowledge retrieval and deterministic escalation checks may run in parallel. A second reviewer is optional only if evaluation justifies it. Join matching case, run, policy, and source versions; preserve conflicts and route material disagreement to a human.

Truth and confidence

Label material claims verified with source, inference with basis, or unknown. Confidence is claim-level and provisional; it does not make claims true or grant authority. Unsupported claims fail validation.

Prototype components

Synthetic fixtures · local SQLite · local versioned retrieval · one model adapter. No production Salesforce, customer email, PHI, or live identity provider without separate approval.

MAO workflow

Each role has a narrow job. The coordinator enforces sequence and the human reviewer retains authority.

One model first
01 · COORDINATOR

Validate & route

Check request shape, permitted fields, workflow, budgets, and stop conditions.

02 · TRIAGE + KNOWLEDGE

Classify & ground

Propose category and urgency; retrieve approved versioned passages with citations.

03 · TROUBLESHOOT + ESCALATE

Suggest & hand off

Offer supported Tier 1 steps, identify missing details, detect must-escalate rules.

04 · DOCUMENT + HUMAN

Draft & decide

Prepare a factual note. Staff edits, approves, rejects, or acts.

Multi-model is deferredCompare another model only if measured quality improvement warrants added cost, latency, data flow, and operational complexity.

Roles, access & safety

Authentication proves identity; authorization limits data and actions by role. Admin configuration rights do not automatically grant unrestricted case or PHI access.

Support Specialist

Reviews permitted tickets and drafts, edits notes, and escalates through authorized processes.

Upper Tier / L2 / Support Lead

Reviews escalations and handles elevated cases under separately granted and audited access.

Administrator

Manages users, roles, queues, SLA/routing rules, and approved knowledge versions.

MAO boundary

No customer replies, credential resets, account or role changes, case closure, or production Salesforce writes.

Prototype data ruleUse synthetic tickets and mock knowledge. Public trial metadata is context, not CDIT support guidance. Keep real customer content and PHI out.
