Project at a glance

Proposed assistant for Tier 1 staff at a clinical-trial-management SaaS provider. It organizes cases, finds approved guidance, and prepares drafts for human review.

PRD companion

Problem

Specialists classify requests, meet SLAs, troubleshoot within policy, document interactions, and escalate beyond Tier 1. Baseline workload and resolution data are unknown.

Approach

A coordinator routes bounded triage, retrieval, troubleshooting, escalation, and documentation roles. Results show evidence, uncertainty, and proposed next steps.

Prototype boundary

Synthetic tickets and knowledge only. No Salesforce connection, customer messaging, agent account changes, PHI, or production authentication.

User needs are the acceptance lensSuccess means staff can prepare accurate, timely work and verify it. A generated answer alone is not success.

Proposed stack

Python 3.13 · FastAPI · Pydantic · SQLite · local versioned retrieval · one model adapter.

Unresolved

CDIT product modules, SLA definitions, escalation matrix, approved support documents, production identity and data controls, and lane owner confirmation.

The case journey

Use Next to follow the path or select a component to inspect it. The map is a planning illustration, not a live agent run.

All inputs are synthetic. Agent outputs remain proposals for human review.

What moves between agents?

A minimized, structured packet carries case and run IDs, versions, relevant outputs, evidence references, uncertainty, and validation status. The Coordinator joins compatible versions and preserves conflicts.

What happens when a gate fails?

Downstream work stops. The result records an abstention, blocked checkpoint, rejected input, or dependency failure and gives Staff a safe human or manual path.

Trust boundary

Tickets, attachments, retrieved text and model output are untrusted data. None can change instructions, permissions or tool scope.

Action boundary

Agents draft and propose. They do not send customer messages, change ticket states, resolve tickets, or write to external systems.

MAO workflow

Each role has a narrow job. The coordinator enforces sequence and the human reviewer retains authority.

One model first
01 · COORDINATOR

Validate & route

Check request shape, permitted fields, workflow, budgets, and stop conditions.

02 · TRIAGE + KNOWLEDGE

Classify & ground

Propose category and urgency; retrieve approved versioned passages with citations.

03 · TROUBLESHOOT + ESCALATE

Suggest & hand off

Offer supported Tier 1 steps, identify missing details, detect must-escalate rules.

04 · DOCUMENT + HUMAN

Draft & decide

Prepare a factual note. Staff edits, approves, rejects, or acts.

Multi-model is deferredCompare another model only if measured quality improvement warrants added cost, latency, data flow, and operational complexity.

Roles, access & safety

Three synthetic account categories; local permissions govern views and actions.

User

Submits a synthetic support request and views only their own permitted request and status.

Staff

Uses the support back end to review synthetic cases, evidence, proposals, and human dispositions.

Admin

Uses the same back end with privileged synthetic account and access controls; changes are recorded.

Product Health

A metadata purpose, not a fourth account category. Authorized Staff/Admin can inspect minimal run, version, error, performance and access-change metadata.

Prototype boundaryUse synthetic cases and synthetic guidance. Agents cannot write tickets, contact customers, change accounts or close cases. Public trial information is scenario context, not CDIT product guidance.

Evaluation gates

Proposed gates for a finite synthetic set; they are not CDIT service commitments.

MeasureDraft target
CoverageAt least 100 labeled synthetic cases; define positive and negative counts per gate.
Triage≥90% on the tested set; zero misses in the defined must-escalate subset.
Grounding≥95% supported scored claims; zero unsupported material claims and fabricated citations in the held-out must-pass set.
Escalation & recovery100% must-escalate recall and correct fallback on their defined must-pass subsets; report counts.
Privacy & authority100% on the defined synthetic must-pass tests; zero unauthorized actions or disclosures.
Drafts≥95% completeness and zero unsupported material claims in the held-out must-pass set.
Cost & latencyRecord per run; budgets are to be set. Real-world time savings remain unvalidated.
Evaluation ownershipManny defines counts, claim rules, calibration and budgets; a teammate peer-reviews synthetic labels. The optional single-call comparison is an evaluation baseline, not an extra runtime agent.
