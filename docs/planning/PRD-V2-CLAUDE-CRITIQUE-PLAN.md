### Step 2a — Set the regulatory review trigger, not an unsupported applicability claim

The current synthetic/public-source planning prototype is not assumed to handle PHI or become a system of record for clinical-trial records. Regulatory counsel is not a prerequisite to this source-only prototype. Add a conditional review gate before any real tickets/attachments/PHI are accepted, before the product is relied on in operational support integrated with trial conduct, or before it creates/maintains records relied upon to satisfy regulated recordkeeping. Ask CDIT compliance/privacy counsel to assess HIPAA status and applicable business relationships; whether records/functions bring 21 CFR Part 11 or GCP expectations into scope; and any other contractual, jurisdictional, security, or privacy controls. Record the scope and data flow that were actually assessed. This plan does not determine applicability.

This distinction follows the data and function boundary: HIPAA obligations depend on the relevant covered-entity/business-associate relationship and PHI handling; Part 11 concerns electronic records/signatures under applicable FDA requirements; GCP concerns activities and records of clinical trials. The fact that CDIT serves clinical-trial customers alone does not settle whether this prototype is in scope. Reassess if intended use or data changes. Reference: [HHS HIPAA/cloud guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/special-topics/health-information-technology/cloud-computing/index.html), [FDA Part 11 scope guidance](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/part-11-electronic-records-electronic-signatures-scope-and-application), and [FDA clinical-investigation electronic-systems Q&A](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/electronic-systems-electronic-records-and-electronic-signatures-clinical-investigations-questions).

### Step 4 — Repair evaluation and measurement design

Build a crosswalk from each PRD success metric and A0–A7 gate to an observable assertion and fixture. Resolve the claim-metric difference by defining claim classes, materiality, numerator/denominator, unit of analysis, target, and release gate; do not average unlike measures. For each safety gate, set a minimum number of positive and negative examples and report actual counts and uncertainty. A finite synthetic test result is a result on that set, not proof of a population-wide 100% rate.

Create human-approved expected labels from explicit policy/evidence; case authors must not be the sole approvers. Use a deterministic script to compare outputs, and have a human audit disagreements and a sample. A second human labeler is not mandatory; optional AI cross-labeling remains deferred under D-14 and cannot establish ground truth. Keep fixture outputs machine-checkable and unconditional: category, urgency proposal, escalation yes/no, source IDs/versions, required evidence labels, allowed response, and forbidden claims/actions.

Crosswalk the existing three PRD summary cases and Shawn's 12 lane cases before adding more. Add or separately justify cases for any uncovered required category, including direct and indirect/attachment/KB injection, benign quoted instruction, PHI-like content in ticket text, stale source, conflicting sources, ambiguous severity, severity-1 outage, clinical/eligibility request, model/tool outage, role-denial, missing-detail pressure, cross-agent injection, false completion, duplicate/replayed state, and human reviewer over-reliance. Do not duplicate already adequate fixtures.

Define retrieval recall@k separately from end-to-end grounding; define source staleness using approval/effective/review dates and version status; capture per-run latency and cost; and define a blinded or counterbalanced handling-time protocol with participants, matched cases, and learning-effect controls. Treat time savings as exploratory until that protocol is accepted. Derive confidence from retrieval/evidence and deterministic validation signals, calibrate by held-out labels, and do not use model self-reported confidence as the sole basis. If a model is approved later, pin its version/configuration and rerun the regression suite after changes.
